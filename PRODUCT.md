# VitalAge — Product Definition

## Challenge

**Nestle Vital VivaTech 2026: "Smart Aging"**

- Deadline: April 27, 2026
- Theme: Small lifestyle shifts in nutrition, physical activity, and mental well-being transformed into sustainable healthy habits
- Focus: Preventive health platforms, holistic health scoring, habit formation

## One-Line Pitch

"60 seconds a day. Your body's user manual, written by your own data."

## Problem

The average health app is abandoned in 7 days. The #1 reason: no feedback loop. You log food, nothing happens. You track mood, nothing changes. There's no connection between what you do and how you feel.

Meanwhile:

- France has 14M+ people over 60, growing to 20M by 2030
- "Silver economy" is a French national strategic priority
- 80% of health apps have <6% retention at day 30
- Existing solutions either require expensive hardware (Whoop $30/mo, Oura $300+) or focus on calorie punishment (MyFitnessPal, 80% dropout by month 2)
- Nobody serves the 45-70 "smart aging" demographic with accessible, positive-framing health tools

## Solution

VitalAge is a daily vitality companion that turns 60-second check-ins into a personal health equation:

1. **MORNING CHECK-IN** (30-60 seconds)
   - Sleep quality (1-5 tap)
   - Energy level (1-5 tap)
   - Mood (tap selection)
   - Any pain or discomfort (optional free text or voice)
   - Anchored to existing morning routine (after coffee)

2. **MEAL SNAP** (throughout the day)
   - Photograph meals, AI extracts nutritional profile
   - Not calorie counting — nutritional quality scoring
   - "This meal is rich in fiber and omega-3" (positive framing)
   - "Consider adding a source of Vitamin C to boost iron absorption" (actionable micro-tip)

3. **VITALITY SCORE** (weekly composite)
   - 0-100 score across 4 dimensions:
     - Nutrition: 72/100
     - Sleep: 85/100
     - Activity: 60/100
     - Mood: 78/100
   - Overall Vitality: 74 (up 3 from last week)
   - Trend graph showing trajectory over time

4. **MICRO-HABITS** (AI-personalized, Duolingo-style)
   - 3 active habits at a time (progressive complexity)
   - "Walk 10 min after lunch" — 12-day streak
   - "Eat 1 fruit before noon" — 5-day streak
   - "Sleep before 11pm" — 8-day streak
   - AI selects habits based on YOUR weakest vitality dimension

5. **AI INSIGHTS** (unlocked after 2-4 weeks of data)
   - Personal correlations from YOUR data:
     - "When you sleep >7h, your energy next day is 40% higher"
     - "Meals with fiber correlate with fewer digestive complaints"
     - "Your mood scores are highest on days you walk >5000 steps"
   - Monthly vitality trend report
   - Shareable with doctor or family

## Target User

- French adults 45-70 focused on aging well
- Health-conscious but not fitness enthusiasts
- Want simple, positive guidance — not punishment or complexity
- May be managing early chronic conditions (pre-diabetes, cholesterol, joint pain)
- Value food culture and quality of life over calorie restriction

## Behavior Change Science (Our Retention Moat)

### Frameworks Implemented

| Framework                     | Implementation                                                                                                                        |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **BJ Fogg Tiny Habits**       | 60-sec check-in anchored to morning coffee. Start absurdly small. Celebrate after completion                                          |
| **Nir Eyal Hook Model**       | Trigger (morning notification) -> Action (check-in) -> Variable Reward (score changed!) -> Investment (your data grows more valuable) |
| **Self-Determination Theory** | Autonomy (choose what to track), Competence (score improves), Relatedness (share with family/doctor)                                  |
| **Implementation Intentions** | Onboarding: "When will you check in? After waking? After coffee?"                                                                     |
| **Loss Aversion**             | Streak system, "your vitality score drops if you skip"                                                                                |
| **JITAI**                     | Just-in-time adaptive interventions — right nudge at right moment based on YOUR patterns                                              |

### Progressive Onboarding (Anti-Dropout Strategy)

```
Week 1-2:   TINY — just mood + energy + sleep (30 seconds)
            NO meal photos. Lower the bar to zero.
            Goal: establish daily ritual.

Week 3-4:   EXPAND — unlock meal photos
            "You've built your rhythm, now add nutrition"
            Progressive complexity, user controls pace.

Month 2:    REWARD — first AI insights appear
            "When you sleep >7h, energy is 40% higher"
            Variable rewards from personal data.

Month 3:    INVEST — first Vitality Report generated
            Shareable with doctor or family.
            Data lock-in: your health history lives here.
```

Industry average retention at day 30: 6%. Our target: 25%+ via friction reduction and personalized insights.
