## One-Line Pitch

60 seconds a day builds your body's user manual — a personal health equation written by your own data.

## The Problem

The average health app is abandoned in 7 days. Not because people don't care about their health — because the apps give nothing back.

You log food. Nothing happens. You track mood. Nothing changes. You record sleep. No insight emerges. The data goes in, but no understanding comes out.

Meanwhile:

- France has 14M+ adults over 60, growing to 20M by 2030
- 80% of health apps have under 6% retention at day 30
- Existing solutions require expensive hardware (Whoop $30/mo, Oura $300+) or punish with calorie counting (MyFitnessPal, 80% dropout by month 2)
- No app serves the 45-70 "smart aging" demographic — the people with the most to gain from preventive health
- The French "silver economy" is a national strategic priority with no digital wellness champion

## Our Solution

VitalAge closes the feedback loop. Every input produces a visible output. After 30 days, the AI knows YOUR body well enough to write YOUR health equation.

### 1. Morning Check-in (60 seconds)

Every morning, after coffee, you answer 4 questions:

- Sleep quality (1-5 tap)
- Energy level (1-5 tap)
- Mood (icon tap)
- Any symptoms? (optional text or voice)

Total time: under 60 seconds. Lower friction than brushing teeth.

### 2. Meal Snap

Photograph your meals throughout the day. AI analyzes nutritional quality — not calories.

- "This meal is rich in fiber and antioxidants"
- "Consider adding a source of calcium"
- Positive framing: what's good, what could be better. Never punishment.

### 3. Vitality Score (0-100)

Weekly composite score across 4 dimensions:

- Nutrition: 72/100
- Sleep: 85/100
- Activity: 60/100
- Mood: 78/100
- **Overall Vitality: 74** (up 3 from last week)

The score moves based on YOUR behaviors. Skip check-ins, it drops. Maintain habits, it rises. One number that tells you "am I trending healthier?"

### 4. Micro-Habits (Duolingo for Health)

3 active habits at a time, AI-selected based on your weakest dimension:

- "Walk 10 min after lunch" — 12-day streak
- "Eat 1 fruit before noon" — 5-day streak
- "Sleep before 11pm" — 8-day streak

Streak flames, progress rings, unlockable new habits. Small changes that compound.

### 5. Personal Insights (Your Health Equation)

After 2-4 weeks of data, AI reveals correlations unique to YOU:

- "When you sleep more than 7 hours, your energy next day is 40% higher"
- "Meals with fiber correlate with fewer digestive complaints"
- "Your mood scores are highest on days you walk more than 5000 steps"

Not generic advice. YOUR body's user manual, written by YOUR data.

## Behavior Change Science (Our Retention Moat)

VitalAge doesn't just use gamification. It implements five peer-reviewed behavior change frameworks:

1. **BJ Fogg Tiny Habits (Stanford)** — Start absurdly small. 60-second check-in. Anchor to existing routine (after coffee). Celebrate completion.

2. **Nir Eyal Hook Model** — Trigger (morning notification) -> Action (check-in) -> Variable Reward (score changed!) -> Investment (your data grows more valuable over time).

3. **Self-Determination Theory (Deci & Ryan)** — Autonomy (choose what to track), Competence (score improves), Relatedness (share with family/doctor).

4. **Implementation Intentions (Gollwitzer)** — Onboarding asks: "When will you check in?" — doubles follow-through in meta-analyses.

5. **Just-In-Time Adaptive Interventions (JITAI)** — Right nudge at right moment based on YOUR patterns, not generic schedules.

### Progressive Onboarding (Anti-Dropout)

```
Week 1-2:   Just mood + energy + sleep (30 seconds)
            NO meal photos. Bar as low as possible.

Week 3-4:   Unlock meal photos.
            "You've built your rhythm, now add nutrition."

Month 2:    First AI insights appear.
            Variable rewards arrive exactly when dropout typically happens.

Month 3:    First Vitality Report.
            Shareable with doctor or family. Investment lock-in.
```

Industry day-30 retention: 6%. Our target: 25%+ through science-backed friction reduction.

## Technical Innovation

- **FHIR R5 Data Model** — Health check-ins stored as FHIR Observations and QuestionnaireResponses. Interoperable with healthcare systems. Doctor can receive structured data.
- **Consent-First Architecture** — GDPR + HDS compliant from day 1. Every AI interaction requires consent, every call audit-logged.
- **Multi-Modal AI** — Mistral Small for check-in analysis and insight generation, Mistral OCR for meal photo interpretation, Voxtral for voice check-ins. European AI (data stays in EU).
- **Correlation Engine** — Custom algorithm detects personal health patterns across nutrition, sleep, activity, and mood dimensions over time.
